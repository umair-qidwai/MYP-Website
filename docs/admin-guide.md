# Website editor guide

For MY Project USA staff and volunteers. No technical knowledge needed.

## Signing in

1. Go to **myprojectusa.org/admin** (there's also a small “Staff sign in” link at the bottom of every page).
2. Enter your email and password. A quick automatic check confirms you're a person; you may occasionally be asked to tick a box.
3. New here? An administrator sends you a one-time link. Open it, choose a password (at least 12 characters — a short sentence works well), then sign in.
4. Forgot your password? Ask an administrator for a reset link. After 5 wrong attempts the account pauses for 15 minutes.

Always use **Sign out** (bottom of the left menu) on shared computers.

## How publishing works

- **Save draft** keeps your changes without showing them to visitors.
- **Preview** shows exactly how the page will look, privately.
- **Publish** makes the changes live, usually right away (sometimes up to a minute).
- Pages show their status: **Draft**, **● Live**, or **Live · unpublished edits** (live, with saved changes not yet published).
- Editors without publishing permission save drafts, and an administrator publishes them.

## Edit a page

1. **Pages** → click the page.
2. The page is made of **sections** (text, picture with text, buttons…). Click a section to open it.
3. Change the words in the text box. Use the toolbar for headings, bold, lists and links.
4. The **Side by side** view shows a live preview next to your edits.
5. **Save draft**, then **Publish** when you're happy.

Useful section controls: ↑ ↓ move, 👁 hide/show, ⧉ duplicate, 🗑 delete. **+ Add a section** adds new content anywhere.

## Update an image

1. Open the section containing the picture → **Replace** → choose from the library or upload a new photo.
2. Fill in **Picture description** — one sentence about what the photo shows (read aloud to blind visitors).
3. Save and publish.

Photos are resized automatically, and hidden location data from phones is removed.

## Publish news

- **Our own article:** Articles → **Add article** → give it a title → write it, add a cover image and date → **Publish**. It appears on the Blog page automatically.
- **Press coverage:** News coverage → **Add news story** → title, outlet, date, link to the story, image, summary → **Publish**. It appears on the News Coverage page.

## Add an event

Events → **Add event** → title → fill in date, time, location, price, image, details, and an optional registration link → **Publish**.
It appears on the Events page and gets its own page. Past events can be **archived** (hidden but kept).

## Upload a report

Reports → **Add report** → title → choose the **Type** (Annual Report, Financial Statement, Tax Return…) and **Year** → upload the PDF in **Document** → optional cover image → **Publish**.
It appears in the matching group on the Reports page. Large PDFs (even hundreds of MB) upload in parts automatically; keep the window open until it finishes.

## Change the menu

*(Administrators)* **Menu** → add, rename, reorder (↑ ↓) or remove items. Each item can have dropdown links. Pick pages from the suggestions in **Goes to**. **Save menu** — it's live immediately.

## Site-wide information

**Site settings**: contact details and footer, Donate/Volunteer buttons, social media links, initiative logos, and an **announcement bar** for urgent news (switch on, write a message, add a link, save).

## Forms and messages

*(Administrators)* **Form submissions** lists messages sent through the website's forms. Open one to read it, archive it, or delete it. **Download spreadsheet** exports them. Submissions can contain personal information, including about young people: only share them with people who need them, and delete what you no longer need.

## Recover from a mistake

- **Not published yet?** More (⋯) → **Discard draft changes** goes back to the live version.
- **Published something wrong?** **History** → pick an earlier version → **Restore this version** → check it → **Publish**.
- **Deleted a section?** Restore an earlier version as above.
- **Deleted a page?** *(Administrators)* Pages → **Bin** → **Restore**.
- **Wrong image?** Images can't be deleted while a page uses them, so nothing disappears by accident.

## Create a new page

Pages → **New page** → title (the web address fills in automatically) → start blank or copy an existing page → add sections → publish → add it to the **Menu** if visitors should find it there.

## People and access

*(Administrators)* **People & access** → **Invite someone** → name, email, role:
- **Administrator** — everything, including people, menu and settings.
- **Editor** — edits content; tick **Can publish** to let them publish.

Copy the link shown and send it to them privately. Use **Manage** to change access, create a password-reset link, sign someone out everywhere, or deactivate an account.

## Good habits

- Write a picture description for every photo.
- Keep headings short and in order (large heading, then small headings).
- Use real, verified numbers only, and say where they come from.
- Link text should say where it goes (“Volunteer application”, not “click here”).
